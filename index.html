The book

A memoir four years in the making

Within weeks of taking the top job, a pandemic closed the parks and the cruise ships. Two years later he was out and his predecessor was back in the chair. This is his account of both, written with New York Times bestselling co-author Don Yaeger.

In 2022 the end came in a weekend, over a phone call, with the board pointing at a quarterly report. Chapek has spent four years working out what he thinks happened. This book is the answer.

What's inside

A memoir in four parts

Thirty years at one company, from a division that sold films on cassette to the chair at the top.

ONE

The videotape years

He arrived in 1993 at a Disney division that sold films on cassette, then carried the business through DVD and Blu-ray and into streaming. Three format shifts that ended other companies, and the rise that followed from them.

TWO

Running the parks

Shanghai Disneyland, Pandora and Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge, and more than $24 billion invested in parks, hotels and cruise ships, a figure the New York Times noted was greater than Disney paid for Pixar, Marvel and Lucasfilm combined.

THREE

The corner office

The pandemic shutdown and the reopening, Disneyland turned into a mass vaccination site, and the pivot to streaming. The decisions that defined the job, told from inside the room.

FOUR

The exit

The weekend in November 2022 when the board replaced him with Bob Iger, five months after a three-year extension. What he thinks happened, and why he waited four years to say it.

The author

About Bob Chapek

Robert A. Chapek spent nearly 30 years at The Walt Disney Company, leading home entertainment, consumer products and the parks before serving as chief executive from 2020 to 2022. This memoir is his first book, written from inside the company he joined in 1993.

He grew up in Robertsdale, the Hammond, Indiana neighborhood beside the Whiting oil refinery, studied microbiology at Indiana University, and earned an MBA at Michigan State before a career in brand management and advertising took him to Disney in 1993. He and his wife Cynthia married in 1981 and have three children. The book was written with Don Yaeger, who has written or co-written more than thirty books, most of them with athletes, coaches and executives.
